# 170: Retire the plan-of-plans into an ADR and a handoff

- **Date**: 2026-10-24
- **Status**: accepted
- **Tags**: plans, orchestration, release, governance
- **Author**: Emiliano Pisu <pisuemiliano.1980@gmail.com>

## Context

`docs/plans/plan-of-plans.md` was the level-0 orchestrator: it measured 150 plan-file instances across five branches, defined the level-1 graph and the stage order, and carried the work from the branch inventory to the RuleMCP rename. Its stages 0-8 are complete, including the `ge`/`dm`/`ex`/`nyx` merges into `feat/evolution`, the Stage 3.5 quick start, the Stage 6 owner handover and the Stage 8 rename. The file is `Status: done` with zero real open checkboxes; the only `- [ ]` is prose describing the ten layout boxes.

The open child plans are a separate concern. They are 10 non-skill plans with 146 open boxes and 86 skill plans with 1,336 open boxes. The plan-of-plans does not claim them as complete, and the reference audit (`docs/plans/plan-of-plans-reference.md`) is retired by ADR 171.

## Decision

Record the plan-of-plans's implemented outcome in this ADR and delete the plan file. Keep the open child plans; handoff 27 indexes them. Delete the plan-of-plans test and its cross-branch helper (`tests/plans/plan-of-plans.test.mjs`, `tests/plans/_cross-branch.mjs`); keep the skills plan test. The plan-of-plans was excluded from the release branch, so the release PR #9 is unaffected.

## Consequences

The level-0 plan no longer exists as a file; this ADR and handoff 27 are the source for its outcome and the open roadmap. The open plans keep their historical links to the retired path, which the release tree already tolerated. The two closed plan files are gone; the 96 open plans remain. The ADR and handoff indexes are regenerated by the docs-numbering doctor.

## What would end this

The plan-of-plans would be needed again if the branch topology or the stage order changed. The observation that would reopen the decision is a new stage that cannot be expressed as an ADR plus a handoff index.

## Alternatives Considered

1. Keep the plan file (it is done and stale; it would compete with the handoff as the source of truth)
1. Delete every plan (the 1,490 open items would lose their detail)
1. Keep only the reference (the audit is retired separately by ADR 171)
