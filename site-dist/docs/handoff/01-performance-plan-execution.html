Where the plan was wrong, and what the measurement said

Three of the plan's numbers did not survive contact with a measurement. All three are corrected in the plan, the audit and the ADRs rather than quietly dropped.

  1. Compression targets of 8% (full) and 20% (ultra) are unreachable on this repository. Its own skills and docs are already written in the compressed style the compression skill teaches, so on them the rules measure how little filler is left, not what they can do. Measured by pnpm report:compression:

    Corpus lite full ultra
    skills (89 SKILL.md) 0.06% 1.52% 2.02%
    docs (17 files) 0.02% 0.56% 0.73%
    real tool results (6) 0.11% 2.03% 2.58%
    verbose prose (synthetic) 17.4% 34.2% 42.3%

    Guard violations are 0 at every level on all four corpora. The floors in src/core/text/__tests__/_efficacy.test.mjs are set under the measured values, with margin, on both the shipped and the synthetic corpus.

  2. NODE_COMPILE_CACHE is worth 2-6%, not the ~35% the plan quoted. The plan's number compared a cold run against a warm one. The cache is still enabled - it is free - but the 168 ms actually came from what --help no longer loads.

  3. The meta rename raised the connect cost. 105 tools and 96,679 tools-list bytes became 106 and 97,204, +525 for the dispatcher. The four entries were renamed, not merged; making them modes of one tool is a wider change and a separate decision.

Bugs the work surfaced

  • A 4-backtick fence wrapping 3-backtick content desynced the fence walker, so docs/04-operations.md had code compressed as prose. Pre-existing; the new guard is what caught it. One CommonMark tracker (_fence.js) now serves the walker, the ultra pass and the guard. Tilde fences were never recognized either, and now are.
  • rule-mcp adr new and fix were dead: they called pix_process_adr and pix_fix, while the registry has carried the rule_tool_ prefix since the rename. Fixed in 0f1a4b1e.
