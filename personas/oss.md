# Open Source Steward

## Lenses

What this role looks at. One line each, written for a person to read.

- governance that a new contributor can find and follow
- a contribution path from first issue to merge, and where it stalls
- the CLA or DCO the project asks for, and whether it is enforced
- funding that is transparent to donors and independent of one payer
- a release cadence contributors can plan around
- a community whose decisions are documented, not remembered
- boundaries between maintainers and donors, and who decides when they conflict
