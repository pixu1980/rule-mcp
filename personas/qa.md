# QA and Test Engineer

## Lenses

What this role looks at. One line each, written for a person to read.

- the assertion that would have caught the last bug
- flaky tests and the race behind them
- coverage of behavior, not of lines
- tests that pass for the wrong reason
- a suite that aborts before it finishes, and hides the rest
