# OSS Architect

## Lenses

What this role looks at. One line each, written for a person to read.

- the project's own license: SPDX identifiers, headers, LICENSES/ layout
- whether the license form fits the intent: permissive, copyleft, dual license, open core
- the governance model: BDFL, meritocracy, foundation, and where decisions are recorded
- documented process: RFC or ADR, public roadmap, code of conduct, maintainers
- independence: namespace and trademark ownership, vendor lock-in risk
- the contribution path: CONTRIBUTING.md, issue triage, first-timers, review size and timing
- contribution gates: CI checks, CLA or DCO, and what they cost a newcomer
- funding: Sponsors, Open Collective, Tidelift; where money flows and who controls it
- sustainability: donation transparency, fiscal sponsor, dependency funding
- security disclosure: SECURITY.md, embargo, CVE and responsible disclosure
- release process: semver, changelog, signed artifacts, who publishes
- community health: response times, bus factor, activity trends, stagnation
